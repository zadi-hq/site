The problem

No bank can show you a card it didn't issue.

A multi-card wallet in the UAE usually spans two or three banks. Each app shows its own slice, so the full picture lives in your head. That is where the money leaks.

Missed due dates

One forgotten date costs AED 230 to 300 in fees, and interest at 30 to 48% APR backdated to the day you spent.

The wrong card

Reward rates differ by card and by category. Paying with whichever card is on top quietly costs real dirhams every month.

Expired rewards

Points usually lapse after 12 to 24 months. Most people find out after it happens, if they find out at all.

The product

One place that understands your whole wallet.

The whole picture, on one screen

Open Zadi and see what a bank app can never show you: everything, together.

  • Combined spend and credit used across every bank
  • Every due date in one list, with reminders that leave time to act
  • Spending by category, month against month

The right card, before you pay

At the till, tell Zadi what you are buying. It ranks your cards for that purchase and shows the answer in dirhams, not points jargon.

  • Category bonuses, caps and card limits all accounted for
  • Miles and cashback compared on real value, so 5x miles stops beating 4% cash on name alone

The money you didn't have to lose

Zadi counts what the wrong card cost you: the gap between the rewards you earned and the rewards your own wallet could have earned.

  • Tracked per category over 30 days, 3 months and 6 months
  • An honest number in AED, so the habit change pays for itself

Arabic, built in from the first version

The entire app mirrors right-to-left: layout, dates, amounts and all. Not a translated menu bolted onto an English app.

Security and trust

Serious about the boring parts.

Your money never touches Zadi

Zadi shows, reminds and recommends. It never holds or moves funds. When in-app settlement arrives, it will run as payment initiation through the UAE's regulated open finance rails.

Nothing to phish

Adding a card takes a name, a limit and a due date. No online banking password, no OTPs, no screen scraping. There is simply no credential to steal.

Your data stays yours

Every record is isolated to your account with row-level security, encrypted in transit and at rest. Your data is not for sale, and deleting your account deletes it.

Built for the regulated era

The Central Bank's open finance framework names credit cards in its scope. Zadi's architecture was designed from day one to plug into those rails as they open.

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Does Zadi connect to my bank account?+

Not yet, and that is deliberate. You add each card in about a minute with its name, limit and due date, and log spending as you go. No banking credentials are involved anywhere. When the UAE's open finance rollout reaches credit cards, live bank data will replace manual entry inside the same app.

Is my money at risk?+

No. Zadi never holds or moves money. It is a view of your cards, a reminder engine and a recommendation tool. Future in-app payments will work as payment initiation through the Central Bank's regulated rails, where the money moves bank to bank and never through Zadi.

What does it cost?+

Nothing during the beta. A Pro tier with the full optimizer and deeper analytics is planned at AED 39 per month once the product is out of beta, and the core view of your cards and due dates will stay free.

Which phones does it work on?+

iPhone and Android, in English and Arabic with a fully mirrored right-to-left layout.

Is Zadi giving me financial advice?+

No. Zadi shows you information about your own cards: dates, rates and reward maths. It does not provide regulated financial advice, and card suggestions are informational comparisons of products you already hold.
