Why now

The UAE is opening its banking rails, and credit cards are already in scope.

The Central Bank's Open Finance framework names credit cards explicitly, alongside accounts, loans and insurance, and participation is mandatory for every licensed institution. It arrives in sequence: accounts and payment initiation went first, with ADCB, CBD, FAB and ADIB certified through 2025 and 2026. Zadi runs on manual entry today by design, which builds the habit early, and the data layer is already abstracted so live bank data replaces manual entry without rebuilding the product. Zadi never holds or moves customer funds.
